The short version

  • Built for macOS only — your clipboard history stays in ClipNest’s sandbox on your Mac.
  • We don’t operate servers that receive your pasteboard content.
  • No accounts, no ads, no third-party analytics or tracking SDKs.
  • We don’t ask for Full Disk Access or Screen Recording.
  • Optional iCloud sync uses Apple CloudKit in your private iCloud container.

1. Scope

This Privacy Policy covers the ClipNest application when you run it on macOS (including Apple Silicon and Intel Macs). It does not apply to other ClipNest products on other platforms unless we link you here explicitly.

By installing or using ClipNest on your Mac, you agree to this policy. If you do not agree, please uninstall the app.

2. Information we collect

ClipNest for macOS is designed to work without an account and without sending your clipboard to us. The categories below are everything the Mac app may handle.

Pasteboard (clipboard) content

When you copy on your Mac, macOS exposes that data through the system pasteboard (NSPasteboard). ClipNest may save items in a local history so you can search, pin, and paste them again. Snippets can include plain text, rich text, URLs, code, file references, and image previews. This content is stored only on your Mac unless you turn on optional iCloud sync.

App settings & metadata

Preferences such as global hotkeys, menu bar behavior, history limits, excluded apps, pinned clips, and appearance are saved locally in ClipNest’s application support folder so your setup persists between launches.

Diagnostic data (only if you opt in)

If you voluntarily send a crash report or feedback email from within the app, we may receive anonymous technical details — for example app version, macOS version, and a crash stack trace. We never attach your clipboard history to these reports.

3. How we use information

Data handled by ClipNest for macOS is used only to run the app:

  • To display and search your clipboard history.
  • To paste previous items when you choose them.
  • To honor your settings, exclusions, and retention rules.
  • To investigate crashes you explicitly submit.

We do not use your data for advertising, profiling, training AI models, or background analytics.

4. Where your data lives on Mac

Clipboard history and settings are stored inside ClipNest’s App Sandbox container — the protected folder macOS assigns to the app, typically under your user Library (for example ~/Library/Containers/ or Application Support, depending on distribution). Other apps cannot read this folder without your explicit permission.

You can clear history from ClipNest’s settings at any time. Uninstalling the app removes its container and local data from your Mac. If you used iCloud sync, see the next section for removing cloud copies.

5. iCloud sync (optional)

ClipNest for macOS can optionally sync your clipboard history across your Apple devices using CloudKit and your iCloud account. If you enable sync:

  • Data is stored in your private iCloud container, not on our servers.
  • We cannot access or decrypt your synced clips.
  • You can disable sync anytime in ClipNest → Settings.
  • Removing data from iCloud may require signing out of iCloud sync in the app and managing storage in System Settings.

Apple’s treatment of iCloud data is described in Apple’s Privacy Policy.

6. macOS permissions

Clipboard managers on macOS need certain system permissions. ClipNest requests only what is necessary and explains each prompt in the app. You can revoke permissions anytime in System Settings → Privacy & Security.

  • Accessibility — often required so ClipNest can paste into other apps and respond to global shortcuts. Without it, some paste and hotkey features may not work.
  • Input Monitoring — on newer macOS versions, may be requested for global hotkeys. ClipNest does not log keystrokes beyond what is needed to register your chosen shortcuts.
  • Login Items / Open at Login — optional, so ClipNest can start when you sign in to your Mac.
  • Notifications — optional, for alerts you enable (for example sync status or history limits).
  • iCloud — optional, only if you turn on sync.

ClipNest for macOS does not require Full Disk Access, Screen Recording, Camera, Microphone, Contacts, or Location. If macOS shows a permission we do not need, please contact us — that would be a bug.

7. Mac App Store

If you download ClipNest from the Mac App Store, Apple processes your purchase, receipt, and account information according to their policies. Apple does not receive your clipboard history from ClipNest. App updates are delivered through the App Store or through our notarized direct builds, depending on how you installed the app.

8. Third-party services

ClipNest for macOS does not embed third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking frameworks. We rely on Apple for the operating system, optional CloudKit sync, code signing, notarization, and (if applicable) App Store distribution.

9. Sharing & selling

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. We do not share your clipboard content with third parties because we never collect it on our servers in the first place.

10. Data retention

Items remain in your local history until you delete them or they age out based on the limit you set in ClipNest. Voluntary crash reports are kept only as long as needed to fix the reported issue, then deleted.

11. Security

ClipNest inherits macOS protections: App Sandbox, Gatekeeper for signed and notarized builds, FileVault disk encryption, and per-user data isolation. Keep macOS updated and use a strong login password. No app can guarantee perfect security, but ClipNest is designed to collect and expose as little as possible.

12. Your rights

Because your data stays on your Mac, you control it directly. You can:

  • Browse, edit, pin, and delete clips in ClipNest.
  • Clear all history or set automatic expiration rules.
  • Turn off iCloud sync and manage iCloud storage in System Settings.
  • Revoke macOS permissions in System Settings.
  • Delete the app to remove all local data in its container.

If you are in the EU, UK, California, or another region with privacy laws (GDPR, UK GDPR, CCPA), you may have additional rights. Contact us below and we will respond within applicable timeframes.

13. Children’s privacy

ClipNest is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has contacted us with personal data, email us and we will delete it.

14. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when ClipNest for macOS changes or when laws require it. We will update the “Last updated” date at the top of this page. Important changes may also appear in the app or release notes.
