Privacy Policy

Pawtone – Dog & Cat Sounds is an iPhone app with a library of original pet sounds, a frequency whistle, a Studio for recording your own sounds and building routines, and a Calm mixer. This policy explains what information the app handles and how. Our approach is simple: Pawtone doesn’t collect your data — everything stays on your device.

The short version

  • No account, no servers, no analytics, no ads, and no tracking — and no third-party SDKs.
  • Your pet’s name, favorites, routines, recordings, and settings are stored only on your iPhone.
  • The microphone is used only when you record a sound in Studio. Recordings never leave your device unless you share them yourself.
  • The app sends nothing to us, so we never see how you use it.
  • Premium and tips are one-time purchases handled entirely by Apple.

1. Information the app stores

Pawtone works without an account and without sending your data to us. The information below is stored on your device so the app can work and remember your preferences.

Pet profile

During onboarding you choose your pet type (dog, cat, or both) and can optionally enter your pet’s name. Both are used only to personalize the app and can be changed at any time under More → Your pet.

Your recordings

If you record a custom sound in Studio, the audio file (up to 30 seconds) is saved on your device together with the name, icon, and color you give it.

Favorites, play counts & recently played

Pawtone remembers which sounds you mark as favorites, how often you play each sound, and what you played recently, so it can show them on the Sounds tab and in widgets.

Routines & whistle presets

Routines you create in Studio (sequences of sounds with repeats, pauses, and volume levels), the whistle frequencies you save, and the last frequency you used.

App settings

Preferences such as haptics, mixing with other audio, theme, and the tab the app opens on, plus whether you’ve completed onboarding and whether Premium is unlocked (so widgets can show the right content).

2. How information is used

Information in Pawtone is used only to provide the app’s features:

  • To personalize the app with your pet’s name and type.
  • To play your recordings and run your routines.
  • To show your favorites, recently played, and most-played sounds.
  • To restore your saved whistle frequencies.
  • To show your sounds and Premium content in widgets and controls.
  • To apply your playback and appearance settings.

We do not use your data for advertising, profiling, or analytics — and because it never leaves your device, we couldn’t even if we wanted to.

3. Where your data lives

Everything above is stored in Pawtone’s private storage on your iPhone: an App Group container that is shared only between the Pawtone app and its own widgets and controls. It is protected by the iOS sandbox and your device passcode, Face ID, or Touch ID. Other apps can’t read it, and we have no server and no copy of your data.

Your data may be included in your device’s iCloud or computer backups if you have those turned on; those backups are managed by Apple and by you, not by us. Uninstalling Pawtone removes all of its data from your device.

4. Microphone & recordings

Pawtone asks for microphone access only when you start your first recording in Studio. The microphone is active only while you are recording — never in the background.

  • Recordings are made and stored entirely on your device. They are never uploaded, analyzed, or sent to us.
  • You can rename or delete any recording in Studio (touch and hold a recording, then choose Delete). Deleting removes the audio file from your device.
  • If you choose Share on a recording, iOS opens the standard share sheet, and the file goes only to the app or person you pick.
  • You can turn microphone access off at any time in Settings → Pawtone. Everything else in the app keeps working.

5. Widgets, Controls & Siri

Pawtone’s Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets and its Control Center controls read your favorites, saved whistle frequencies, and Premium status from the shared App Group container on your device. When you tap one, the sound plays through Pawtone on your iPhone — nothing is sent anywhere.

Siri and Shortcuts support is built with Apple’s App Intents framework. Your voice requests are handled by Siri under Apple’s privacy policy; Pawtone only receives the resulting action, such as which sound to play, and carries it out on your device.

6. Permissions we request

Pawtone asks for just one permission, and only when you need it:

  • Microphone — optional, to record custom sounds in Studio.

Pawtone doesn’t request access to your location, contacts, photos, camera, or notifications, and it never asks to track you across other companies’ apps and websites.

7. Purchases & tips

Pawtone is free to download. Pawtone Premium is an optional one-time purchase that unlocks every sound, the full whistle range, and unlimited recordings and routines. There are no subscriptions. You can also leave an optional tip in the Tip Jar.

All purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store, and the app checks your Premium status on your device using Apple’s StoreKit. We never receive your payment details, name, email address, or Apple ID. Restore purchases simply asks Apple to confirm purchases made with your Apple Account.

8. Third-party services

Pawtone contains no third-party advertising, analytics, crash-reporting, or tracking SDKs. It relies only on Apple frameworks built into iOS:

  • AVFoundation — to play sounds, generate whistle tones, and record your custom sounds, all on device.
  • StoreKit — for Premium, tips, restoring purchases, and the optional App Store rating prompt.
  • WidgetKit & App Intents — for widgets, controls, Siri, and Shortcuts.

If you have chosen to share analytics with app developers in your iPhone’s settings (Settings → Privacy & Security → Analytics & Improvements), Apple may provide us with aggregated crash and usage statistics that don’t identify you. This is controlled by Apple and by you. Apple’s handling of data is described in Apple’s Privacy Policy.

9. When you contact us

If you email us — for example with Send feedback in the app — we receive your email address and whatever you choose to include in your message. We use it only to reply to you, never add you to a mailing list, and delete the conversation once it’s no longer needed.

10. Sharing & selling

We do not sell, rent, or trade personal information. There is nothing for us to share: your pet profile, recordings, and usage stay on your device and are never sent to us.

11. Data retention

Your data stays on your device until you delete it or uninstall Pawtone. You can delete recordings and routines in Studio, remove favorites, reset play counts under More → Stats, and change or clear your pet’s name under More → Your pet.

12. Security

Pawtone relies on the protections built into iOS: app sandboxing, encrypted storage, and your device lock. Because the app has no servers, there is no online database of your information that could be breached.

13. Your rights

Because your data stays on your device, you control it directly. You can:

  • View, edit, and delete your recordings, routines, favorites, and pet profile in the app.
  • Reset play counts at any time.
  • Allow or revoke microphone access in Settings → Pawtone.
  • Uninstall the app to remove all of its data from your device.

If you are in the EU, UK, California, or another region with privacy laws (such as the GDPR, UK GDPR, or CCPA), you may have additional rights. Since we don’t hold data about you, most requests can be handled right in the app — but you are always welcome to contact us, and we will respond within the applicable timeframes.

14. Children’s privacy

Pawtone is suitable for all ages, but it is not directed at children under 13. The app doesn’t collect personal information from anyone, including children — a pet name entered in the app stays on the device. If you believe a child has sent us personal information by email, contact us and we will delete it.

15. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when Pawtone changes or when the law requires it. We will update the “Last updated” date at the top of this page, and significant changes may also be noted in the app’s release notes.
