Getting started

Pawtone has five tabs:

  • Sounds — 51 original sounds in seven packs. Tap to play, touch and hold to loop, and star your favorites.
  • Whistle — a tone generator from 100 Hz to 22 kHz with Steady, Pulse, Sweep, and Warble modes.
  • Studio — record your own sounds and build routines.
  • Calm — layer soothing loops and set a sleep timer.
  • More — Premium, your pet profile, settings, and the widgets guide.

Using sounds safely

  • Start at around 50–70% volume and only go louder if you need to.
  • Watch how your pet reacts, keep sessions short, and stop if they seem stressed or scared.
  • Don’t hold your iPhone right next to your pet’s ears — especially with the whistle at high frequencies.
  • Pawtone is for play, training, and comfort. It isn’t a substitute for advice from your vet or a professional trainer.

Frequently asked questions

Why can’t I hear the whistle at high frequencies?

That’s normal. Many adults can’t hear tones above roughly 15–17 kHz, while dogs and cats hear much higher. iPhone speakers also play very high tones more quietly. Watch your pet’s reaction rather than relying on your own ears.

What’s free, and what does Premium unlock?

The free version includes a selection of sounds from every pack, the whistle up to 12 kHz, 3 recordings, and 2 routines. Pawtone Premium is a one-time purchase that unlocks every sound and calm layer, the full whistle range up to 22 kHz, and unlimited recordings and routines. There are no ads in either version and no subscription.

How do I add a widget?

  1. Touch and hold your Home Screen until the apps jiggle, then tap Edit → Add Widget.
  2. Search for Pawtone and pick the Sound Board (1, 4, or 8 buttons) or the Whistle widget.
  3. To choose which sounds appear, touch and hold the widget and tap Edit Widget. Otherwise it shows your favorites.

You can also add a Pawtone widget to your Lock Screen. A step-by-step guide is in the app under More → Widgets, Controls & Shortcuts.

Can I use Pawtone from Control Center or the Action button?

Yes, on iOS 18 and later. Open Control Center, tap +, then Add a Control and choose Pawtone (Play Sound, Clicker, or Stop All). On iPhone 15 Pro and later, go to Settings → Action Button → Controls → Pawtone.

Does Pawtone work with Siri?

Yes. Try “Play a sound in Pawtone”, “Blow the whistle in Pawtone”, or “Stop sounds in Pawtone”. The same actions are available in the Shortcuts app.

Can sounds play over my music or podcast?

Turn on More → Mix with other audio. When it’s off, Pawtone pauses other audio while it plays.

Do Calm sounds keep playing when my iPhone is locked?

Yes. Calm sounds loop in the background until you stop them, and the sleep timer fades them out gently when time is up.

How do I change the app’s language?

Open Settings → Pawtone → Language on your iPhone, or tap Language in Pawtone’s More tab. Pawtone is available in English, German, French, Dutch, Italian, and Russian.

Recordings & routines

How do I record my own sound?

In Studio, tap New recording, hold your iPhone near the sound, and record up to 30 seconds. Then give it a name, icon, and color. Pets often respond best to familiar sounds — try recording yourself calling their name.

How do I rename or delete a recording?

In Studio, touch and hold the recording, then choose Rename or Delete. Deleting removes the audio file from your iPhone permanently.

What is a routine?

A routine plays a sequence of sounds with repeats, pauses, and a volume level for each step — for example, to slowly get your pet used to the doorbell. Studio includes ready-made templates to start from.

Troubleshooting

I can’t hear any sound

Turn up the volume with your iPhone’s volume buttons while a sound is playing. Pawtone plays even when silent mode is on. If you use Bluetooth headphones or a speaker, open the Now Playing bar in the app to check where the audio is going.

Recording doesn’t start

Pawtone needs microphone access to record. Go to Settings → Pawtone and turn on Microphone, then try again.

My widget doesn’t show my latest favorites

iOS refreshes widgets on its own schedule. Opening Pawtone usually updates them right away; if not, remove the widget and add it again.

Premium & purchases

Pawtone Premium is a one-time purchase — no subscription. To restore it on a new or reset iPhone, open More → Restore purchases while signed in with the same Apple Account you used to buy it.

Tips from the Tip Jar are one-off thank-yous and don’t unlock anything, so they can’t be restored. Payments, receipts, and refunds are handled by Apple — you can request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com.
