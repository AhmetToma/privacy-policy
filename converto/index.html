Privacy Policy

Converto converts and views files on iPhone, iPad and Mac: PDFs, images, spreadsheets, CSV, JSON, Markdown, HTML, text and more, plus a set of PDF tools. Every conversion runs entirely on your device. This page explains what Converto handles, where it lives, and the rights you have.

The short version

  • No account, no sign-in, no ads, no analytics, no tracking.
  • Files are converted on your device. They are never uploaded anywhere.
  • We don’t run servers that receive your data, so we never see it.
  • Purchases are handled by Apple; we never see your payment details.

1. Information Converto handles

Converto only works with the files you give it. We, the developer, do not collect any of them.

Your files

  • Files you open: documents you pick from Files or Finder, drag in, share to Converto, or open with “Open in Converto”.
  • Photos: only the images you pick to convert, through the system picker.
  • Scans: pages you capture with the document scanner, turned into a PDF on your device.
  • Converted files: the outputs you create, saved in Converto’s Library.

Settings

Preferences such as the app language and whether you have seen the welcome tour are saved on your device.

What we don’t collect

Converto contains no analytics, advertising or crash-reporting SDKs. We don’t collect identifiers, location, contacts, usage data or diagnostics. If you choose to share crash reports with developers in your device settings, Apple may pass anonymized reports to us under Apple’s own terms.

2. Where your files live

Files you open are copied into Converto’s private app container so they can be read and converted, protected by the operating system’s app sandbox and data protection. Converted files are saved to Converto’s Documents folder, which on iPhone and iPad is also visible in the Files app under “On My iPhone/iPad → Converto”. Nothing is synced or uploaded by Converto.

Markdown and HTML files are previewed with scripts turned off.

3. Purchases and the free trial

Converto Pro is sold through the App Store as a monthly or yearly subscription or a one-time lifetime purchase. Apple processes the payment and manages subscriptions; we never receive your name, Apple Account or payment details. Converto checks your purchases with Apple’s StoreKit on your device.

To count the free conversions in the trial, Converto keeps a single number in your device’s Keychain. It contains no personal information and never leaves your device.

4. Permissions

  • Camera (iPhone and iPad): only when you scan a paper document into a PDF.
  • Photos: only the pictures you pick, through the system picker.
  • Files: only the files you choose to open, and the place you choose to save to.

5. Third-party services

The only services Converto relies on are Apple’s: the operating system and the App Store, including in-app purchases.

6. Sharing & selling

We do not sell, rent, trade or share your personal data. We never receive it, so there is nothing for us to share. Files leave Converto only when you share, save or drag them out yourself.

7. Data retention & deletion

Converted files are kept in the Library until you delete them. Copies of files you opened are temporary and cleared by the system. Deleting Converto removes everything it stored on that device.

8. Security

Converto relies on Apple’s security model: app sandboxing and device encryption. PDFs you protect with a password are encrypted on your device. Keeping your device updated and protected with a passcode keeps your files protected too.

9. Your rights

Because your files stay on your device, you already control them: you can view, share or delete them at any time. Users in the EU, UK, California and similar jurisdictions have rights under laws such as the GDPR, UK GDPR and CCPA. We hold no personal data about you, but if you have a question or request, contact us and we’ll help.

10. Children’s privacy

Converto is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

11. Changes to this policy

If Converto starts handling data differently, we’ll update this page and the “Last updated” date above before the change ships.
