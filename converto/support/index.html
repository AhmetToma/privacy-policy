Getting started

  1. Open a file: pick it on the Convert screen, drop it onto the window (Mac and iPad), or use “Open in Converto” from another app.
  2. Choose what to turn it into. Converto only offers conversions that work for that file.
  3. Find the result in the Library, then share it, save it or drag it out.

Frequently asked questions

Which formats does Converto support?

To PDF from images, text, Markdown, CSV, JSON, Excel, HTML, Word and RTF; from PDF to images, text, RTF and CSV tables; CSV, JSON and Excel to each other; Markdown to and from HTML; and images between common formats.

What are the PDF tools?

Merge, split, extract, delete and reorder pages, rotate, add or remove a watermark, protect with a password and unlock. Converto can also unzip archives.

Are my files uploaded?

No. Every conversion runs on your device, and Converto works without an internet connection.

Where are my converted files?

In the Library. On iPhone and iPad they are also in the Files app under “On My iPhone/iPad → Converto”.

Converto Pro

Converto includes free conversions to try it. Converto Pro unlocks unlimited conversions and tools on iPhone, iPad and Mac with one purchase: monthly, yearly or lifetime.

  • Restore a purchase: open the upgrade screen and tap Restore Purchases, signed in with the same Apple Account.
  • Cancel a subscription: Settings → your name → Subscriptions on iPhone or iPad, or App Store → your account → Subscriptions on Mac. Cancel at least 24 hours before it renews.
  • Refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Troubleshooting

  • A file won’t convert: it may be damaged, password-protected or in an unsupported variant. Try opening it in another app and exporting it again.
  • A protected PDF won’t open: use the Unlock tool with its password first.
  • The scanner can’t use the camera: allow camera access in Settings → Privacy & Security → Camera.
  • A zip won’t extract: password-protected archives aren’t supported yet.
